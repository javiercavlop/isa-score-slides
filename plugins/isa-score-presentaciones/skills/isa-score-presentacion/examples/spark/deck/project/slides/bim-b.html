BIM v1, as documented in the repository, splits the same content into a package of separate, reusable resources. A small core, instance.json, identifies the instance and references its resources. The profile qos-binding/v1 is the contract for this family of problems: exactly one application, one or more candidate catalogs, zero or more constraint sets and exactly one optimization. Dialects add compatible vocabularies: BPMN, placement (qos-binding-placement/v1) or a routing overlay. Everything is validated and transformed into one common representation for the engines.
