SPHERE lets us design, edit, validate, analyse, version and publish iPricings. SPACE, the Subscription and Pricing Access Control Engine, manages subscriptions and evaluates what each customer can use, given the applicable pricing and the usage state. It issues a signed pricing token with access information.
