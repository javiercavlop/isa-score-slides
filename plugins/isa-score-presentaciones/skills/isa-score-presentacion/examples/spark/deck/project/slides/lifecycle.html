Publishing is the step with operational meaning

31

Pricing-Driven DevOps uses pricing models along development and operation. SPHERE lives on the development side: design, validate, version, publish. SPACE lives on the operation side: subscriptions, access and usage. The renderer shows the offer and the app enforces it. Editing a draft and publishing a version are different operations: only publishing changes anything for customers.
