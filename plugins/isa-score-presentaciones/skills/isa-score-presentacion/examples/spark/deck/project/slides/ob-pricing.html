Closing the loop, from the OpenBinding documentation (Plans and Limits). SPACE is OpenBinding's authoritative engine for subscription contracts, feature evaluation and metered consumption; SPHERE hosts the releases of OpenBinding's iPricing. The gateway runs pre-flight checks against SPACE and reserves a concurrency slot; a background worker reports consumed solver seconds and releases the slot. Plans: a free default tier, RESEARCH through University of Seville authentication, ADVANCED and PRO through agreements. Limits such as solver seconds per month (900 s in BASIC, 180,000 s in PRO) or concurrent jobs (1 to 8). Exceeding them yields 402, 429 or 403 responses.
