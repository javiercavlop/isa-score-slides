Three distinctions to keep when we show results. A problem incompatible with one engine can be valid and solvable with another. Not finding a solution within the available time does not prove none exists. Engines differ: not all accept the same formulations or guarantee optimality. OPTIMAL, FEASIBLE, INFEASIBLE and UNKNOWN describe solver termination and do not weaken validation.
