Three layers, as documented in the OpenBinding docs. Access: BIM v1 packages, the web workbench and the REST API, and the platform where projects, studies and reports live, addressed by immutable identifiers and SHA-256 digests. Gateway: resolution and validation, canonical lowering to the BindingProblem IR, compatibility check against the engine's capabilities, dispatch to an exact engine revision, and a trust boundary where the gateway independently re-evaluates the result. Engines: local or federated (bim-engine/v1, with an EngineRegistration).
