Who does what. Delivera is the logistics operations app and owns its billing screen; not everything there comes from the renderer. pricing-renderer shows the pricing and the current contract, previews changes and shows a summary before confirming. SPACE already validates when modifications are saved, so the integration with SPACE has a first real use. Connecting the full SPHERE publication cycle is the extension still to come.
