QACOBench is the collection of problems for evaluation; QACOForge is the configurable generator of new instances; OpenBinding provides common execution. Together they let us reuse problems from several domains, generate new cases and study how engines behave. A comparison is only meaningful with explicit conditions: compatibility, constraints, objectives, execution budgets and, for randomised methods, variability across seeds.
