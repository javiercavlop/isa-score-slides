IoT: connected devices that collect data or act. Edge: resources close to the device or the user. Cloud: remote infrastructure. FaaS deploys functions whose execution and resources can be billed by consumption. In CLASP-FaaS we decide where each function of a composition runs, weighing cost, latency and security.
