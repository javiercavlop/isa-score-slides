A QPU is a quantum processor; a simulator runs the simulation on classical resources. Candidates are resource–provider offers, because the same resource can come with different conditions from different providers. Quantum Resource Binder builds the selection instances and uses OpenBinding to solve them. Three things to keep apart: picking a QPU does not make the selection engine quantum, it does not show quantum advantage, and selecting resources is a different decision from distributing shots.
